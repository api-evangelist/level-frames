# Level Frames

> Level Frames is a New York City based online custom framing company (founded 2014, Y Combinator W15) that lets customers design and order handcrafted, made-to-order picture frames from home. Level Frames publishes no public API, developer portal, or SDK — this file describes a consumer storefront, not a developer surface.

## About

- [Level Frames](https://www.levelframes.com/): Online custom framing storefront — upload a photo to print and frame, or ship in artwork, posters and prints for handcrafted made-to-order framing.
- [Pricing](https://www.levelframes.com/pricing): Published pricing guide for frame sizes, materials and options.
- [Customer gallery](https://www.levelframes.com/gallery): Examples of completed framing work.
- [Blog](https://www.levelframes.com/blog): Company blog.

## Products

- Custom frames for art prints, posters and physical artwork
- Photo printing and framing from uploaded digital images
- Framed canvas prints
- Framed vinyl records
- Collage frames and gallery walls
- Personalized framed photos
- Framed chalkboards, whiteboards and corkboards
- At-home (DIY assembly) and in-studio framing services

## API status

Level Frames has no public API. As of 2026-07-19 there is no developer portal,
API reference, OpenAPI/AsyncAPI definition, SDK, CLI, MCP server, or webhook
surface published by the company; `api.`, `developer.` and `docs.levelframes.com`
do not resolve, `/api` redirects to the homepage, and no `/.well-known/`
discovery documents are served. The help center's "Artist Partnerships" and
"Framing for Businesses" programs are commercial arrangements, not API products.
